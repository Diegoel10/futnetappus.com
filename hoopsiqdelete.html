Delete Your Hoops IQ Account

Applies to the Hoops IQ app on Android and iOS

You can ask us to delete your Hoops IQ account and the data connected to it at any time. Here's how.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to diegoponcefl88@gmail.com with the subject line "Hoops IQ account deletion".
  2. Include the email address you use to sign in to Hoops IQ (or say that you signed in with Apple or Google, and which email that account uses).
  3. We'll confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days. You'll get an email once it's done.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details (name, email address, user ID)
  • Your game data: display name, coin balance, streaks, scores and progress
  • Your purchase records stored in Hoops IQ

Once deleted, your progress, coins and purchased items can't be recovered.

What may be kept

  • Purchase receipts are kept by Apple or Google, and by our purchase provider RevenueCat, as required for tax, accounting and fraud-prevention purposes. These are handled under their own retention policies.
  • Advertising and diagnostic data collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is not linked to your account and is retained by Google under its own policies.
  • Backups may hold your data for up to 30 additional days before being permanently removed.

Questions

Contact us at diegoponcefl88@gmail.com. For more on how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.
