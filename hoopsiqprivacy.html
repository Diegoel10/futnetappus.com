Hoops IQ Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hoops IQ ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our basketball trivia app on iOS and Android. By using Hoops IQ, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. When you sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive basic account details such as your name, email address, and a unique user ID. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive only Apple's private relay address.

Game data. We store information related to your gameplay, such as your display name, coin balance, streaks, scores, and progress, so your account works across sessions and devices.

Purchase information. In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive or store your payment card details. We receive confirmation of what you purchased so we can deliver your coins or items.

Device and usage information. Like most apps, Hoops IQ and our service providers may automatically collect information such as device type, operating system, app version, crash reports, general usage activity, and advertising identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and save your progress
  • To deliver in-app purchases and restore them when needed
  • To show advertisements in the App
  • To fix bugs, improve performance, and develop new features
  • To respond to support requests
  • To prevent fraud, cheating, and abuse

3. Third-Party Services

Hoops IQ relies on trusted third-party services that may collect and process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (authentication, database, and app services) — Firebase Privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (in-app purchase management) — RevenueCat Privacy
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases) — Apple Privacy
  • Google (Google Sign-In, Google Play purchases) — Google Privacy Policy

4. Advertising and Tracking

Hoops IQ displays ads through Google AdMob. Ads may be personalized based on your device's advertising identifier. On iOS, we will ask for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before using your advertising identifier for tracking; if you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be less personalized. Where required by law (for example, in the European Economic Area and the UK), we will ask for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can also limit ad tracking at any time in your device settings.

5. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers listed above to operate the App, when required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the App.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We keep your account and game data for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time from within the App (where available) or by emailing us at the address below. Once deleted, your progress, coins, and purchases cannot be recovered.

7. Children's Privacy

Hoops IQ is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard services and safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, or to object to certain uses of it. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
